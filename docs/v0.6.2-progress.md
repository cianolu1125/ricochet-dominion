- Dedicated geometry-only Charge III mask, frozen enemy tower/pioneer targets and atomic full-map mutation.

Reduced motion removes camera translation and shake while preserving the rule result. Resize and interruption cleanup restore normal interaction.

## Decisions
- Physical flight timing is preserved. Camera chase duration follows actual flight; the post-impact cinematic is 1.66s, reduced motion 0.70s. This prevents effects from changing projectile physics.
- Territory victory retains the existing complete-round check rather than introducing a mid-action victory rule; HP victory remains immediate in authoritative state and visually deferred through the ultimate.
- Background interruption settles presentation immediately to the already committed final map; it never reruns rules.
