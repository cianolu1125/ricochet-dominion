## Implementation

- Battlefield and HUD occupy separate flex rows. The camera fits only the battlefield row with a four-pixel rim. The HUD is 56px on phones, 60px on touch tablets, 58px on desktops, plus safe-area where required.
- Short role-anchored branch nodes fade and slide 7px in 170ms. Links derive exclusively from currently visible nodes. Disabled actions are dim, not dashed. Enemy role is the information entry point; card times out after 2400ms plus a 140ms exit, and hides immediately for aim/transition.
- End Turn directly starts a locked handoff. Touch: 400ms rotation, 200ms observation pause, exactly one beginTurn, actual settlement effects, then input. Desktop/reduced motion skip rotation but retain observation pause. No-change turns add no artificial settlement delay.
- Rule instrumentation emits facts with unique eventId and causal groupId; the director retains every independent bounce/damage and merges only simultaneous causal groups. No cooldown or sound queue drops real events. Engine HP facts are recorded at each decrement, including multiple carry crossings within one step.
- One WebAudio context, generated short noise buffer and synthetic tones; shared compressor, bounded waveshaper, master volume and priority ducking. Voices disconnect on completion. No music/haptics.
- Canvas fire/launch rings, short trails, three sparks per bounce, outline flash and individual damage numbers, eight-fragment tower destruction, retract/build deployment, I/II/III capture particles (6/10/16, 190/250/330ms), capped relay confirmation (120ms), carry arc and dedicated siege impact.
- Ground transitions use shared time/distance data. Rise, fall and landing color switch affect only rendering. Existing role/tower coordinates stay unchanged. 3×3 wave <=405ms, 5×5 <=470ms, siege wave <=520ms, large conversion <=610ms; stable/temporary status changes keep rule colors.
- Sound defaults on at 65%; reduced motion follows system until explicit user choice. Settings persist. Reduced motion disables camera shake, reduces transient particle budget from 80 to 24, and uses 90ms color fades. Backgrounding clears effects/audio backlog and physics accumulator.
