## Scope and implementation

Offline Easy / Normal / Hard PvE, human Red versus computer Blue; Quick / Standard / Long remain 10 / 14 / 18 rounds. No rule/stat/action bonuses, perfect execution, new map, campaign, tutorial, online mode or cloud AI. Local PvP retains its phone hot-seat rotation and controls. `engine.js`, `physics.js`, `charge.js`, `outposts.js`, `claims.js` are byte-identical to the V0.6.2 baseline; `config.js` changes only the version.

Search uses cloned states and the authoritative legal executor and physics, isolated in a module Worker. Main applies one real decision, shows its perturbed aim for 260ms, waits for the actual animation/settlement and replans. Every launch/relay has centered nonzero error from an independent execution stream. Difficulty budgets are 260 / 1,250 / 5,000 nodes; a physical segment is bounded to 600 ticks, a relay chain to five distinct captures plus release. Hard considers local refinement, full own-turn uncertainty, complete bounded opponent relay chains, selective remaining actions and real next-turn growth. Bounded future infrastructure value reflects unused growth capacity rather than inflating old mature towers.

The controller validates generation, request and state tokens, locks human tactical input during AI turns, preserves the human phone view, cancels obsolete workers and clears the PvE session on explicit stop. Overlay/background pauses replay a full fresh cue before execution resumes. Errors/watchdogs end the real turn legally; there is no main-thread search fallback. Existing charge, carry, rebound, shielding, overload, takeover/reclaim, pending, ultimate, audio and result flows remain authoritative.

## Independent review and corrections

The whole-branch reviewer found no Critical rule/PvP/isolation defect. The two Important search issues were reproduced in failing regressions, then corrected: own-turn uncertainty omitted the selected follow-up/end, and the opponent reply horizon stopped after only two launch segments. Full paths now replay to their reported ideal value; a fresh five-relay response removes three protected enemy towers through real Charge III physics. Branch allocations reserve capacity across relay depth.

Additional failing checks led to explicit session clearing on stop and pending takeover ownership prospect evaluation. Search-selection fixtures now cover distant takeover, reclaim, redeployment and dismantling. Calibration exposed an old Hard maturity multiplier that discouraged useful redeployment; it was replaced by bounded unused stage/mask growth, respecting protection, board limits and remaining rounds. The initial four-match calibration is retained as historical evidence in the raw lab record. Two completed pre-closing-fix losses are retained as investigation evidence; their strong HP but weak area outcomes led to a closing-score regression. Final two-round evaluation now follows area, HP, then outpost count, while terminal victories dominate. A cross-length regression also exposed fractional-HP elimination bias: nine of eighteen HP incorrectly attracted finishing priority. Hard now uses actual remaining HP for that desire, while preserving real terminal simulations. Interrupted runs are excluded from final strength evidence.

## Practical verification boundary

Physical iOS/Android touch comfort, audible sound quality, device FPS and battery/thermal behavior are unverified: this environment provides automated phone layouts and real Chrome, not physical devices or audible monitoring. These items are never marked passed. Audio lifecycle and Reduce Motion are covered by the automated suite.

Hard's eight-second wall-clock cutoff can limit search on slower devices; each cutoff is recorded separately from illegal execution or a stuck match. Searches return their best bounded plan or the controller uses a legal end-turn fallback while the separate Worker protects UI responsiveness. Broader seeds and human playtests remain useful balance work.
