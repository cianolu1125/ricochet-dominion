## Architecture and behavior
Search isolated structuredClone states inside a module Web Worker; return one decision, never a final state. Replan at every real decision point (including relay capture). Atomic search follows chains through up to five different outposts with bounded beam search; compare Move→Action and Action→Move. Hard adds local refinement, independent error robustness and selective opponent response. Terminal outcomes dominate finite normalized features. Analyze territory, stable/temporary, HP, infrastructure, influence, pending, overload, relay, safety and round pressure. Strategies dynamically blend expansion, network, disruption, siege, elimination, recovery, takeover and endgame.

Execution angle and power errors are nonzero in all difficulties, seeded independently from search randomness. Generate fresh match seed on restart/rematch. Tie choices and simulations are reproducible. Search cannot preview execution RNG. Real animation and physics run normally, including ultimate; show actual aim for 260ms before launch.

## Concrete implementation contract

| Setting | Easy | Normal | Hard |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Node ceiling / decision | 260 | 1,250 | 5,000 |
| Relay beam width | 3 | 6 | 12 |
| Semantic targets / action kind | 12 | 20 | 32 |
| Own-turn first-action shortlist | Immediate | 3 | 6 |
| Selective opponent turn plans | 0 | 0 | 4 |
| Execution uncertainty samples | 0 | 3 | 7 |
| Angle sigma, degrees | 5 | 2.5 | 1 |
| Power sigma | .065 | .035 | .016 |

Angle/power errors use centered Gaussian samples clamped to 2.5 sigma. Actual power is clamped to the engine's [.08,1] legal range. Search samples never read or consume the execution stream. Every move/missile/relay release is perturbed at execution, including Hard. Local refinement improves ideal inputs; it never corrects a flying body's actual trajectory.

Simulation has a 600-tick bound and shares original engine/physics. An atomic chain has at most five distinct captures and its final release (six segments). Beam allocations reserve capacity across depth rather than consuming everything at the first layer. The eight-second wall-clock limit is a safety cutoff; the normal search ceiling is the node budget. Controller failure/watchdog terminates the Worker and legally ends the AI turn. No synchronous main-thread search fallback is permitted.

Normal compares both Move→Action and Action→Move, and every actual result triggers fresh planning. Hard appends the complete selected second atomic path and final settlement to its uncertainty samples. Its opponent model searches complete atomic relay chains, then selective remaining turn actions and actual next-turn settlement. All final contenders receive comparable response/risk budgets. Replanning accepts missed captures and other physical error; subsequent plan segments are never forced into an incompatible state.

Baseline normalized feature weights remain territory 2.6, stable 1.3, HP 1.8, outposts 1.2, influence .7, pending .6, relay .5, safety .4, overload −.6 and temporary liability −.25. Terminal win/loss is ±1,000,000; normal heuristic scores cannot outrank victory. Strategy weights alter priorities, not rules. Hard's construction horizon estimates unused structural/mask growth over at most two remaining owner turns, respects enemy protection and board bounds, and vanishes at the limit. Already mature structures receive no invented structural maturation bonus. In the final two rounds, Hard uses a bounded lexicographic score projection: area first, then HP, then total owned outpost count, exactly matching engine settlement. One cell outweighs every nonfatal HP/outpost advantage; terminal HP/60% victories still dominate. Earlier rounds retain the strategic normalized evaluation. Pending contested ownership belongs to the incoming captor in evaluation, with defender-proximity risk; actual tower ownership remains controlled only by engine settlement.

Menu: choose existing match length, VS Computer, then Easy/Normal/Hard and Start. Rematch preserves mode/difficulty/length and changes seed. During AI turn, tactical menus, dragging, tower selection and End Turn are disabled; permitted settings remain available outside existing presentation restrictions. The 260ms cue shows the perturbed actual vector. Overlay/background pause clears it and resumes with a complete new cue. Human Red stays on the same viewing side; local PvP still uses original hot-seat handoff.

Worker messages carry generation/request identity, cloned state and controller session. Reply contains one decision/strategy/diagnostics, never a final map. Main validates generation, request and state key, executes through original APIs/feedback, waits for flights/settlement/ultimate, and requests a fresh decision. Explicit stop disables the session as well as terminating Worker/cue/pending work.
